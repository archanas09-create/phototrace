A FIELD GUIDE / THREE QUIET STEPS

A closer look,
on your terms.

PhotoTrace helps you understand the extra details a photo may carry — then lets you decide what to do with them.

01

BEGIN WITH A PICTURE

Choose

Select a JPEG, PNG or WebP from your device. PhotoTrace checks that it can read the file and shows you a preview before anything else.

↗
02

READ WHAT'S THERE

Discover

Look for camera, capture date, image dimensions and GPS coordinates. EXIF details come from the original file; file size and resolution are identified separately.

⌕
03

KEEP THE MOMENT

Choose

Keep the original, or create a separate Canvas-encoded copy without the source EXIF metadata. The new image can look slightly different after re-encoding.

✳
PLEASE NOTE

A missing GPS tag is not proof that a photo is completely private. The scene itself — faces, signs, landmarks and context — may also reveal information.
