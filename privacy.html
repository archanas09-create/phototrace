Leaving or reloading the app discards its in-memory photo
WHAT THE APP USES

Only what the page needs.

The selected file is read by the browser for local EXIF analysis and Canvas re-encoding. PhotoTrace does not request camera, location, or other device permissions. The EXIF reader and fonts load from third-party CDNs; those requests do not include your selected photo.

The app also removes any photo left in the legacy PhotoTrace IndexedDB store from an earlier version. If that cleanup is blocked by another open PhotoTrace tab, close the older tab and reload this page. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

!

A note about browser security. Keeping a photo in page memory avoids persistent browser storage, but it does not protect against a compromised device, malicious browser extensions, or other software with access to your browser. Close the page on shared devices.
