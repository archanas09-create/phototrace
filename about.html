A NOTE FROM PHOTOTRACE

Keep the picture.
Know the rest.

PhotoTrace is a small, independent photo utility built around one simple thought: before you share a picture, it helps to know what else is traveling with it.

MADE TO STAY LOCAL

Your photo is read and processed in this page's memory. There is no account, upload service, or photo storage. When you leave or reload the PhotoTrace app, its in-memory photo and generated copy are discarded.

MADE TO BE CLEAR

PhotoTrace distinguishes details read from EXIF from information derived from the image file. Missing fields are shown as unavailable, not guessed.

MADE TO GIVE YOU A CHOICE

If you want, create a separate re-encoded image without the original EXIF. The first file is left unchanged; visual quality or format characteristics can shift a little.
